Mitochondria — The Alien Inside You

How a 2-billion-year-old deal created all complex life

July 4, 2026

School mein padha hoga - "Mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell" But kya aapko pata hai ki yeh mitochondria actually ek alag organisms hai jo humari cells ke andr rehta hai? Chalo detail main janate hai.

Kahani shuru hoti hai 1886 main. Richard Altmann naam ke ek German scientist ne microscope mein cells ke andar kuch ajeeb granules dekhe. Usne bola yeh koi granules nahi hai, yeh khud jeevit organism hai jo humare cells ke andar rehte hai. Logo ne uska mazak udaya, uska career barbad ho gya. Bichara sahi tha, bas 100 saal pahle bol diya.

Abh aata hai twist, 1967 mein Lynn Margulis naam ki ek American scientist ne ek paper likha. Usne bola yeh mitochondria pahle free-living bacteria the, yani humare cells ke baher inki duniya thi. Lekin 2 billion saal pahle ek badi cell ne inhe kha liya but digest nahi kiya. Dono ne deal kar li - cell ne bola - "main tumhe ghar dunga, tum mujhe energy do"

Uska paper 15 journals ne reject kiya. Logo ne kaha yeh pagal hai. But phir 1980s mein DNA sequencing aayi. Scientists ne dekha ki mitochondria ka apna DNA hai jaise bacteria ke pass hai. Lynn Margulis bilkul sahi thi.

Toh soch ke dekho - aap jo saans le rahe ho, woh trillions ex-bacteria ko feed kar rahi hai jo aapke andar rehte hai. Aap akele nahi ho - aap ek chalti firti bacterio ki colony ho.

Aur agar 2 billion saal pehle woh ek weird symbiotic event nahi hota, toh na plants hote, na animals, na hum.

Har cheez jo hum dekhte hai, kisi na kisi prakar evolution se judi hue hai.

Agar aapko is topic mein aur details mein janna hai, to aap yeh kitab padh sakte ho.
